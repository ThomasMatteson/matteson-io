      The invention

      The rules that let AI
      carry responsibility.

      MTech gives AI a reliable way to act on its decisions inside a real business, where money moves, equipment ships and people are accountable.

      MTech’s architecture keeps every piece of work tied to an owner, an authority and a record as it passes between AI agents, people and systems. An agent proposes. A separate control layer checks the approval, the current record and who owns the step, then acts and records what happened. Unclear answers from the outside world are reconciled before anything is repeated.

      About these diagramsThe figures on this page are explanatory illustrations.
      FIG. 1 · The central mechanism

      The protected transition

      When work moves from one agent to another, the transfer of responsibility and the exact approved next action are committed as one step. Either both happen or neither does.

      The new owner receives the work with its authority, evidence and obligations attached. The previous owner can no longer act on it.

      One owner · one approved next step · one record

      See it step by step
      FIG. 2

      Authority separation

      The agent states what it intends to do. A separate control layer holds the credentials and acts only when current authority, a matching approval and the live record all agree.

      Permission lives in the control layer

      FIG. 3

      Recovery from uncertain outcomes

      When a supplier or bank stays silent, the system marks the outcome as unknown and checks the outside record before any action is sent again.

      Reconcile before replay

      FIG. 4

      Ownership and version checks

      Each part of a job has one current owner and a version. An agent working from an old view, or one that no longer owns the step, is turned away before it can act.

      One owner per concern

      FIG. 5

      Approval bound to the action

      An approval covers one exact, recorded action and is used once. Change the item, the amount or the destination and it is a different action that needs a fresh approval.

      One approval, one exact action

      FIG. 6

      Evidence before money

      Billing and payment depend on the state of the work. An invoice cannot move ahead of the completion evidence it relies on.

      Work state governs financial state
