Four stages

From first practice
to everyday work.

Each stage teaches by doing, and each one is something you can try right here.

Stage 01 · VR training

Rehearse the real job before the real site.

Crews practice in a virtual site with the same in-lens guidance they’ll see through the glasses on the floor. Mistakes cost nothing, and the first real job feels familiar.

Stage 02 · Coworker training

They learn how you work, then ask when unsure.

Digital coworkers learn from the way your team already works: your past jobs, your procedures and your corrections. When something is unclear, they ask once and remember.

Stage 03 · Implementation

Start by watching. Grow by trust.

Coworkers go live alongside your team, suggesting before they do anything. You decide when each one moves up a level, and you can move it back at any time. Rollout moves at your pace.
