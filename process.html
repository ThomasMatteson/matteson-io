Every step finishes with proof.

Every step of a process has one owner, a deadline and the evidence that proves it was done. A step closes only when its proof is attached.

How a step closes

Owner. Work. Proof.
Done.

Every step in a process follows the same path. The evidence requirement is built into the step from the start.

Assigned

One named owner and a due time, set when the step is created.

Worked

The owner, a crew or a digital coworker does the work.

Proven

The required evidence is attached: a photo, a reading, a signature.

Verified

The evidence is checked against what the step requires.

Closed

The step closes and the next owner is handed the work.

Try it

Close a step.
Then try to skip one.

A sample service job. Attach the evidence and close each step, or try closing without it. Let the clock run and see what happens to work that slips.

      Evidence gates

      A step cannot close, and the next cannot start, until the required proof is recorded.

      Ownership

      One named owner

      Each step has one owner. Moving it to someone else is a recorded transfer.

      Deadlines

      Deadlines that act

      Late work is flagged and escalated the moment it slips.

      Record

      A complete record

      Who did what, when, and with what proof, from the first step to the last.
