MTech/The job

Intelligence that participates in the work.

MTech brings the knowledge and coordination of an entire organization to the moment work happens: to the person at the machine, to the team around the job, and to whoever is running the business.

FIG. J1 · Two worlds, one job
The problem

A company’s knowledge
is scattered.

Some lives in manuals. Some lives in software. Some is buried in email. A tremendous amount lives in experienced people’s heads.

Manuals

Procedures and specs

Written once, filed somewhere, rarely open at the moment they matter.

Software

Records and history

Assets, work orders and service history, spread across systems that don’t talk.

Email and chat

Decisions and context

What the customer said, what was promised, what changed, buried in threads.

People

Experience

Which units act up, which sites need extra care, what really fixed it last time.

In the field

The person standing in front of the machine usually has access to only a fraction of it.

In the office

Meanwhile, the office coordinates the job with incomplete information about what is actually happening.

MTech connects those two worlds.

Three parts, one system

See the work. Carry it.
Keep it connected.

Each part does a different job. Together they let physical work become business information, and business information become coordinated action.
